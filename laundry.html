How it works

  1. Pick two coins. The top one is what you pay. The bottom one is what you get.
  2. Type how much. You see what a plain swap gives you.
  3. Swap fee range. The washer can take a little or add a little. Red shows the most it can take. Green shows the most it can add.
  4. Press Swap. Your wallet asks you to say yes. Then the washer spins.
  5. Safe by design. Signing in never moves money. A swap asks your wallet to send only the amount you typed. We never ask for your secret phrase.
  6. Recent in this menu shows every real swap. Tap one to check it on Solana.
  7. See the odds shows the math. C0de opens the real code on GitHub.
  8. Bubble rewards. Pop bubbles for a future reward coming 12-24-26. Connect your wallet so your pops count. Every wallet gets 200 free bubbles to start. Your count is in this menu.
  9. Other chains. Tap the coin you pay with, then pick Ethereum, Base, Robinhood or BNB, or search a token by name. Each one shows its chain. You pay from that chain and get any coin on Solana. It lands in your Solana wallet as SOL, ready to swap.
  10. Invite. Share your link from this menu. A friend who joins gets 300 bubbles and so do you. Their first swap gets you 500 more. Every 3 friends who swap gives you 7 days of no fees on swaps.
  11. Vending machine. Swipe to the side to find it. A pull costs 100 bubbles. Most pulls give a candy bar just for fun. Now and then one gives a real g00b NFT to claim. Swipe back for the washer.

Hidden fun

  • Press the ¢ button to roll the least and most swap fees again.
  • The coins in the washer keep changing.
